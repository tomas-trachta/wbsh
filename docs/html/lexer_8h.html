Detailed Description

POSIX shell tokenizer.

Scans raw program text into a vector of Token values, capturing the structural boundaries of quoting and $-expansion as a sequence of WordSegment values. The bodies of expansions ($(...), ${...}, $((...)), etc.) are stored as raw text and re-parsed by later stages — the lexer's job is to find their extents, not to interpret them.

Heredoc bodies are also captured here: when a << operator and its delimiter word are seen, the lexer remembers the delimiter and, on the next newline, slurps lines until the delimiter is matched.
